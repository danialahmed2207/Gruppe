# Team Communication in IT

> Best practices for effective communication in technical teams.

---

## Communication Channels

| Channel | Best For | Response Time |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| **Ticketing System** | Work tracking, incident records | Based on SLA |
| **Email** | Formal announcements, documentation | Hours to days |
| **Instant Messaging** | Quick questions, coordination | Minutes |
| **Video Calls** | Complex discussions, retrospectives | Scheduled |
| **Documentation Wiki** | Knowledge sharing, runbooks | Asynchronous |

## Writing Good Tickets

A well-written ticket saves time and reduces back-and-forth.

### Template

```
**Summary**: Brief, specific description

**Environment**: OS, application, location

**Steps to Reproduce**:
1. Step one
2. Step two

**Expected Result**: What should happen

**Actual Result**: What actually happens

**Impact**: Who is affected and how severely

**Attachments**: Screenshots, logs, error messages
```

## Incident Communication

When systems fail, clear communication is critical.

### Internal Updates

- Notify the team immediately in the incident channel
- Post regular updates every 15–30 minutes during active incidents
- Include: what happened, what is being done, expected resolution time

### External Updates (if applicable)

- Acknowledge the issue promptly
- Provide realistic timelines (under-promise, over-deliver)
- Post-resolution summary with root cause

## Escalation Guidelines

| Severity | Response Time | Escalation Path |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| P1 Critical | Immediate | Senior engineer → Manager |
| P2 High | 30 minutes | Team lead → Senior engineer |
| P3 Medium | 4 hours | Self-resolve or team discussion |
| P4 Low | Next business day | Standard queue |

## Remote Work Best Practices

- Over-communicate status and blockers
- Document decisions in writing
- Use video for complex discussions
- Respect time zones and working hours
- Share progress visibly (daily updates, standups)

---

## Key Takeaway

Technical skills get you hired. Communication skills determine how effective you are in a team.
